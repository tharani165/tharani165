## About Me

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate (2026) who moved into data engineering the practical way — by building a full pipeline from scratch before ever being taught one. My internship at **Kaar Technologies** has me porting real ETL workflows into standardized Prefect + FastAPI + React architecture; outside that, I build IoT and computer-vision systems that solve physical-world problems, from fall detection to flood monitoring.

I care about pipelines that are boring in the best way — predictable, observable, and don't wake anyone up at 2am. My background in embedded systems means I think about data engineering the way I think about hardware: constraints first, elegance second.

**Currently open to:** Entry-Level Data Engineer roles · Data pipeline / ETL engineering · Analytics engineering
